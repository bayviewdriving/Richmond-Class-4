Why Bayview Makes it Easier to Pass the First Time?

Reading the manual only tells you "how to check"; taking our lessons is about mastering "fast and accurate" test strategies. We simplify the Pre-trip Inspection into a 10-minute workflow, helping you win the examiner's confidence with a perfect score and a professional "Safety-First" impression from the start.

Exclusive Pre-trip "Keyword Mnemonics" & Workflow Simplification

The core of our strategy is analyzing exactly what keywords examiners expect to hear (e.g., pointing to the Coolant tank and saying "Level enough, Secure, No Leak"). We train you to identify components and immediately state the "must-hear" results. Our goal is a perfect Pre-trip score, establishing your professional authority before you even leave the parking lot.

Best-in-Class Inspection Sequences for Your Vehicle

Step-by-step demonstrations of seamless inspection sequences tailored to your specific vehicle type (e.g., 4-door sedan vs. 5-door hatchback vs. 7-passenger SUV). We cover critical model-specific details, such as the inspection order for rear seatbelts and sliding doors in 7-passenger SUVs, as well as the precise location and operation of Car Headlights (Low/High beam) and the Parking Brake (Handbrake). Mastering these sequences ensures you perform naturally and confidently during the ICBC road test.

Personalized Driving Habit Analysis Based on ICBC Standards

A comprehensive evaluation of your personal driving habits against official ICBC scoring criteria to provide a personalized roadmap for success. I will closely monitor your driving and identify potential point-deduction triggers (e.g., single-handed steering, rolling stops, or missed shoulder checks) and serious errors that lead to automatic failure. You will receive professional guidance on ICBC-compliant techniques, including proper steering posture, Right of Way judgment, Speed Control, and 360-degree safety observations before reverse parking.
